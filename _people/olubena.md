I am an M.S. Geoscience student and Graduate Research Assistant in SHOAL Lab, completing my thesis with Dr. Jeremy Patterson. My research uses the tidal signals in groundwater levels to characterize the aquifer beneath Bald Head Island, NC. I combine field monitoring of wells and surface water with groundwater modeling to estimate aquifer properties and look for heterogeneity. I am especially interested in how coastal aquifers respond to tides and saltwater intrusion, and what that means for municipal water supply. Outside of research, I am involved in student leadership and community service at UNCW and enjoy exploring coastal life in Wilmington.
